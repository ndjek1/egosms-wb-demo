Connected WhatsApp accounts

Connect a client's WhatsApp Business Account and phone number through Meta Embedded Signup.

#{empty account.verifiedName ? 'WhatsApp Business Account' : account.verifiedName}
Business ID: #{empty account.businessId ? 'Not returned' : account.businessId}
Phone number ID: #{account.phoneNumberId} #{account.wabaId} #{account.connectionStatus}

What happens when you connect an account?

  1. Meta asks the logged-in client to select or create their business portfolio, WABA and phone number.
  2. egoSms securely exchanges the one-time authorization code on the server.
  3. The WABA is subscribed to this app's webhooks and the phone number is registered.
  4. The resulting access token is encrypted and assigned only to the currently logged-in egoSms user.
